DROOBI — ENGINEERING NOTES
==========================

Current focus
-------------
- Keep campus navigation behavior isolated from the Home navigation flow.
- Treat GPS quality and route availability as separate diagnostics.
- Do not let a temporary compass reading issue silently end an active session.
- Make arrival detection explicit and based on repeated location evidence.
- Keep user-facing guidance understandable when location data becomes unreliable.

Review reminders
----------------
- Distinguish "session active" from "guidance temporarily unavailable".
- Record why startup fails instead of reporting every failure as a routing issue.
- Verify that rerouting failures do not accidentally terminate navigation.
- Check subscription cleanup when a navigation screen or service is disposed.

These are engineering notes and review points, not claims that the items above
have all been implemented or tested.
